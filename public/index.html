How to play

Everyone starts with 21 tiles and builds their own crossword grid. Every row and column of two or more letters must be a word, and all your tiles must join up into one grid. You can rearrange your grid any time.

Peel: when every tile in your hand is on your grid, tap Peel. Everyone draws one new tile, including the bots. When a bot peels, you get a tile too.

Dump: stuck with a hard letter? Drag it onto Dump (or select it and tap Dump). It goes back in the pool and you draw three. Only while the pool has 3 or more.

Finish: once the pool has fewer tiles than players, the first to use every tile wins. Against bots, any bad word at that moment means you lose. Solo, you just get told what to fix.

Words: anything in the ENABLE list (about 168,000 words, close to the Scrabble list), two letters and up. No names or abbreviations.

Controls

  • Drag tiles from your rack onto the board, around the board, back to the rack, or onto Dump.
  • Type a word: tap an empty square (tap again to switch across/down), then tap rack tiles. The cursor skips over letters already there. ⌫ takes the last one back.
  • Tap a rack tile then a square to place it.
  • Drag empty space to move around; pinch to zoom; ⌖ re-centres.
  • On a keyboard: type letters, Backspace, arrows, and Space to switch direction.
